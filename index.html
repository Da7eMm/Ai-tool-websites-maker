Central Nervous System

Frontal Lobe

Lobus frontalis

English Description

C1 Level

The frontal lobe is located at the front of the brain and is associated with reasoning, motor control, emotion, and language (Broca's area). It is crucial for high-level executive functions and decision-making processes.
